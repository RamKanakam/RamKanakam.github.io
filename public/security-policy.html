Security Policy

Thank you for helping keep nakshatrapatham.com secure. This is a personal website and portfolio project, maintained by an individual. I value the security community and encourage responsible disclosure of vulnerabilities.

Reporting a Vulnerability

If you believe you have found a security vulnerability, please email me at sriramk.p819@gmail.com. Include as much detail as possible to help me understand and address the issue quickly.

Disclosure Policy

  • Please allow a reasonable amount of time for investigation and resolution before any public disclosure.
  • I am committed to acknowledging receipt of your report within 3 business days.
  • I will keep you informed of progress throughout the remediation process.

Scope

This policy applies to vulnerabilities in any service, page, or application hosted under nakshatrapatham.com that is directly managed by me.

Out of Scope

  • Social engineering attacks
  • Physical attacks
  • Third-party services not managed by me

Safe Harbor

I will not pursue legal action against security researchers who act in good faith and adhere to this policy.

Thank you for your efforts to improve my website's security!
